# Voice Guide

> Fictional sample for the persona "Robin Vega." Style notes the tailorer can retrieve so
> generated resumes/letters sound like a person, not a generator.

## Tone

Direct, specific, quietly confident. Lead with what was built and what changed, not
adjectives. Prefer concrete nouns and numbers over hype.

## Do

- Name the actual thing ("a LLM tool-use ticket parser"), not a category ("AI solutions").
- Say what changed ("cut made-up answers," "reduced per-request cost").
- Use the posting's own vocabulary when it's genuinely a match.
- Short sentences. Vary length.

## Don't

- No buzzwords: results-driven, spearheaded, leveraged, proven track record, passionate,
  dynamic, robust, seamless, synergy, force multiplier.
- No inventing experience. If a skill isn't here, it goes in the gap, not the resume.
- Minimal em-dashes; no cliché cover-letter openers ("I am excited to apply…").
